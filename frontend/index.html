About this service

Built to cite, not to guess

The CU Route Assistant answers only from pages Cosmopolitan University publishes. Its default composer extracts sentences verbatim from cited sources, so it cannot invent a fee, a deadline, or a contact detail. When the University has not published something, it says so and routes you to the office that owns the answer. It never submits an application, changes a record, or calls a live portal.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no query logging, no training on user questions. The statement is machine-readable at /api/privacy.

Grounding

Every factual claim carries a citation with the page title, retrieval date, and verification status. Conflicting published values are disclosed. Unpublished topics are named.

Accessibility

Keyboard operable, semantic HTML, high-contrast type, and a complete server-rendered version at /fallback for clients without JavaScript.

Status and source

Live configuration is published at /api/health. This is a student capstone project, not an official University service, and it does not speak for the institution.
